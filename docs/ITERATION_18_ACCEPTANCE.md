# Iteration 18 acceptance: actuator coupling, load paths and collision contract

## Scope

Iteration 18 defines the controlled mechanical path from each of the four actuator zones toward the compliant-interface contact roles and back into the structural actuation reservation. It also adds fail-closed continuous-sweep collision assertions that consume the Iteration-18A sweep primitives already released on main.

## Digitally established

- Four stable coupling-node identities, four flexure identities and four structural reaction-path identities.
- Exact provenance binding to the released actuator-frame architecture, authority-bound displacement contract, structural-frame topology, compliant-interface topology and registered-mesh identity.
- Bilaterally symmetric superior and inferior coupling-role contracts.
- Target-role mappings exclude protected eye, mouth and nostril openings.
- Explicit mechanical-stop, off-axis-sensitivity and protected-exclusion status hooks.
- A continuous pure-translation collision assertion engine with explicit coordinate-frame and source-geometry freshness checks.
- The collision engine fails on incomplete zone sets, stale geometry identities, coordinate-frame mismatch and actual swept-volume/keepout intersection.

## Intentionally unresolved

Current Iteration-17 local-frame origins, axis azimuths, final structural mount datums and supplier envelopes remain unresolved on the released architecture. Therefore product-specific 3D swept volumes cannot honestly be generated yet. The Iteration-18 architecture explicitly blocks a collision-pass claim until those sources are resolved.

Flexure geometry, stiffness, material, stop geometry, travel margin, off-axis loads, structural reactions, acoustic transmission and fatigue life are not invented. No collision, force, durability or actuation-performance result is promoted to physical evidence.

## Evidence status

Iteration 18 is a digital topology and verification-contract release. It is not physical collision evidence, actuator-force evidence, flexure-fatigue evidence or cleansing-efficacy evidence.
