# Scholarship evidence matrix

[Scholarship review snapshot](SCHOLARSHIP_REVIEW_SNAPSHOT.md) · [Venture progress](VENTURE_PROGRESS.md) · [Customer discovery](CUSTOMER_DISCOVERY.md) · [Visual evidence quicklook](VISUAL_EVIDENCE_QUICKLOOK.md)

This page gives a non-technical reviewer a direct path from common entrepreneurship-scholarship questions to inspectable Masck One evidence. It is an evidence map, not a claim that the venture has completed customer, physical or commercial validation.

| Reviewer question | Evidence available now | Where to inspect it | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Is this more than an idea? | Controlled requirements, code-generated parametric engineering, automated checks, design records and whole-product integration work exist in the repository. | [Engineering authority](../config/masck_one_authority.yaml), [engineering source](../src/masck_one/), [tests](../tests/), [convergence review](CORE_SKETCH_CONVERGENCE_REVIEW.md) | Digital development does not prove physical performance, safety, comfort or manufacturability. |
| Has the founder shown initiative? | The repository history records a progression from controlled engineering foundations to cross-system integration and then to an external-review evidence structure. | [Scholarship review snapshot](SCHOLARSHIP_REVIEW_SNAPSHOT.md#evidence-of-initiative) and [evidence guide chronology](EVIDENCE_GUIDE.md#verifiable-repository-chronology) | Commit volume is not treated as proof of venture quality or independent manual authorship. |
| Is there customer thinking? | A founder-reported informal survey of around 20 people provides a preliminary signal around time and convenience, with concerns around comfort, maintenance and price. | [Customer discovery](CUSTOMER_DISCOVERY.md) | The underlying questionnaire, recruitment method and raw response data are not in the repository. The result is preliminary and non-conclusive, not evidence of demand or willingness to pay. |
| Is the first product scope disciplined? | The broader wearable-and-dock direction is separated from the immediate task of identifying one routine use case where customer value, physical feasibility and plausible economics overlap. | [Venture progress](VENTURE_PROGRESS.md#product-vision-versus-first-validation-target) | The first use case is deliberately not locked until stronger customer evidence exists. |
| Does the founder understand the main risks? | Customer, fit, fluid, hygiene, manufacturing and economic uncertainties are stated explicitly and tied to evidence needed next. | [Venture progress](VENTURE_PROGRESS.md#evidence-maturity-at-a-glance) and [commercial uncertainty register](VENTURE_PROGRESS.md#commercial-uncertainty-register) | Recording a risk does not resolve it. Several important questions require physical testing or external expertise. |
| Is there a credible next stage? | The next sequence is structured customer interviews, focused simulation and analysis, a focused physical fit prototype, core fluid-delivery testing, hygiene and cleaning investigation, and initial manufacturing-cost modelling. | [Scholarship review snapshot](SCHOLARSHIP_REVIEW_SNAPSHOT.md#what-happens-next) | These are planned evidence-generating activities, not completed milestones. |
| Would scholarship support change the evidence base? | Support is framed around customer validation, prototype materials and test equipment, manufacturing and cost investigation, and specialist human collaboration. | [Venture progress](VENTURE_PROGRESS.md#evidence-gated-use-of-scholarship-support) | No funding amount, technical outcome or customer outcome is assumed. |
| How is AI used? | AI is used extensively to accelerate implementation, research, alternative generation and engineering exploration. Founder judgement owns direction, requirements, priorities, trade-offs and final decisions. | [Scholarship review snapshot](SCHOLARSHIP_REVIEW_SNAPSHOT.md#founder-and-ai-roles) | AI-assisted analysis is not treated as independent physical evidence. Specialist human expertise remains important for hardware, manufacturing, IP, fundraising and market entry. |

## The venture case in one sentence

Masck One has substantial inspectable digital development and a disciplined evidence trail, while the customer problem, first use case, physical performance and commercial case still need to earn validation before the broader product vision expands.
