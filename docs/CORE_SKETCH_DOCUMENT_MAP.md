## Canonical work order

For any broad Masck One product task, read in this order:

1. `CORE_SKETCH_START_HERE.md` — canonical entry point and work-selection rule.
2. `PRODUCT_CONCEPT.md` — highest-level whole-routine thesis, boundaries and portability definition.
3. `CORE_SKETCH_ONE_PAGE.md` — one-minute product invariant test.
4. `CORE_SKETCH_V1.md` — full selected product/UX/industrial-design specification.
5. `CORE_SKETCH_FIVE_LANE_EXECUTION_PLAN.md` — current five top-level missions and routing of the previous subsystem-centric five tasks.
6. `CORE_SKETCH_STATUS_BOARD.md` — compact view of current P0/P1 gates and evidence state.
7. `CORE_SKETCH_EXECUTION_BACKLOG.md` — dependency-ordered step-by-step product-wide to-do list.
8. `CORE_SKETCH_REPO_MAPPING.md` — maps backlog work to established owner lineages and genuinely new owner areas.
9. The relevant subsystem engineering documents, live machine/brand authority, current owner PR/branch and accepted exact-head evidence.

Do not start from the research archive when implementing. Research records exploration; it does not select product truth.

## Truth hierarchy

The Core Sketch describes the selected target product and how work should converge toward it. It does not override live engineering truth.

When statements conflict, use this order for claims about what physically exists or works:

1. released machine engineering authority and schemas;
2. accepted exact-head source/CAD/evidence;
3. released subsystem engineering documents;
4. Core Sketch selected product intent;
5. research archive and historical exploration.

A concept target may deliberately demand future engineering change, but that conflict must remain explicit until the engineering is changed and revalidated.
