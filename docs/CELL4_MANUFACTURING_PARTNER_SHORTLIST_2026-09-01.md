# Cell 4 manufacturing partner shortlist

Evidence date: 2026-09-01. Region priority: Australia first, then global scale partners.

Status: PUBLIC-EVIDENCE SHORTLIST ONLY. No company below is an approved supplier, has been contacted, has accepted MASCK ONE, or has been qualified for this product. Certification statements are limited to the cited public company material and must be reverified for the exact site/process before qualification.

## Selection logic

MASCK ONE needs a staged supplier strategy rather than a single premature contract-manufacturer commitment. The practical sequence is: local/high-access NPI and electronics support for alpha builds, specialist rapid plastics/LSR tooling for geometry and material learning, then integrated CDMO/EMS evaluation once interfaces, CTQs, test methods and volumes are controlled. This reduces transfer risk while avoiding an early production freeze.

## Ranked shortlist

### 1. Planet Innovation, Melbourne, Australia

Public evidence: https://planetinnovation.com/manufacturing/

Publicly states contract development and manufacturing for healthtech products and consumables, transfer-to-manufacture through at-scale production, facilities in Melbourne and Irvine, ISO 13485:2016 and ISO 9001 compliant manufacturing, ISO 7 cleanrooms, wet labs and warehousing.

Apparent MASCK ONE fit: strongest Australia-based candidate for integrated NPI/CDMO discussion after digital-alpha closure because the public capability set spans regulated instruments, consumables, assembly and manufacturing transfer.

Potential role: integrated alpha-to-pilot manufacturing partner, DFM/NPI review, assembly/test architecture, consumable process review.

Unknowns to close: willingness to engage a pre-revenue consumer wellness program; minimum program size; cosmetic consumer-hardware finishing capability; LSR/compliant-interface process ownership versus subcontracting; fluidic leak/flow test capability; battery pack integration policy; tooling ownership; NRE structure; forecast/MOQ expectations.

Advance gate: NDA plus non-confidential fit call, followed by evidence for exact proposed manufacturing sites/processes, example NPI gate structure, quality deliverables, tooling ownership terms and prototype/pilot commercial constraints.

## Recommended producer sequence after digital-alpha closure

1. Contact Planet Innovation with a non-confidential system brief. Ask whether its Melbourne team is appropriate for an electromechanical wearable with clean-fluid storage, contaminated-waste consumable, compliant face interface, battery electronics and functional test. Require an explicit proposed scope, site/process map, NPI phase gates and information required for a formal quotation before disclosing detailed IP.
2. In parallel, send Circuitwise a bounded electronics NPI package and Reid Print Technologies a bounded HMI/flexible-interface coupon package. Keep responsibilities separated so neither supplier silently becomes authority for the whole product.
3. Send Protolabs only geometry/material learning parts and coupons where rapid tooling has high information value. Do not interpret rapid-molding success as production-process qualification.
4. After alpha CTQs, failure modes, assembly sequence, test methods and pilot volumes are known, compare Planet Innovation against Jabil/Flex-class integrated manufacturing paths. Selection must be based on the exact proposed sites/processes, NRE/tooling ownership, quality evidence, transfer plan, supply-chain resilience and commercial fit, not brand scale.

## Minimum non-confidential introduction package

Provide: product category and intended use without efficacy claims; system block diagram; approximate envelope/mass target ranges; major material/process families; fresh/waste fluid separation concept; battery/electronics architecture summary; replaceable-consumable concept; alpha/pilot quantity ranges as planning ranges only; target markets; expected prototype sequence; requested supplier role; required NDA before controlled CAD, detailed routes, algorithms or unpublished architecture.

Do not provide before NDA and scope fit: detailed CAD, proprietary route geometry, tolerance stack, firmware/source code, unpublished test methods, supplier-specific cost model, unreleased industrial design surfaces or patent-sensitive mechanism detail.

## RFQ / NPI questions

Require each candidate to identify the exact legal manufacturing entity and site; in-house versus subcontracted processes; applicable certifications and scope; DFM/DfX deliverables; tooling ownership and maintenance; material lot traceability; incoming/in-process/final inspection; CTQ capability-study options; leak/flow/electrical functional-test capability; firmware programming/serialization; battery sourcing and transport responsibilities; silicone/plastic material documentation; change-control process; nonconformance/CAPA handling; prototype, EVT/DVT/PVT or equivalent build sequence; MOQ/forecast assumptions; NRE/tooling/payment structure; lead times; IP/data handling; disaster recovery; and end-of-life/last-time-buy handling.

## Physical-validation procurement gates

Before integrated alpha procurement, Cell 4 should have controlled test requirements for reservoir usable volume and pickup/orientation behavior; cleanser compatibility and purge; fresh-route dead volume and prime/purge; pump pressure-flow with representative water and cleanser viscosity ranges; mixed-phase waste acquisition/backflow/occlusion/full-cartridge faults; cartridge sealing and service contamination; wet/dry ingress boundaries; battery fault/swelling clearance; charging and peak-current behavior; thermal paths; cavity drain/dry behavior; and service trajectories.

Every purchased component remains REFERENCE_CANDIDATE until exact manufacturer part number, revision/datasheet provenance, package geometry, environmental/material compatibility, electrical/fluid operating envelope and incoming verification are bound into controlled evidence.

## Claims firewall

Supplier marketing and prototype success do not authorize claims of leak-proof operation, universal cleanser compatibility, guaranteed cleansing efficacy, exact runtime, skin-safe WARM temperature, orientation-independent waste recovery, waterproof electronics, production readiness or regulatory approval. Those require product-specific evidence.
