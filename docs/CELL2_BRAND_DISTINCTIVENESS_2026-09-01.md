# Cell 2 brand distinctiveness pass, 2026-09-01

Status: digital-identity red-team record. This is not trademark clearance and does not freeze a physical product mark.

The straight split-field v1 failed because two equal vertical masses converged toward a pause/control glyph. The flowing equal-field v2 fixed the straight channel but retained two similarly weighted tall panels. Source and raster spot checks at 16 and 64 px still left material two-panel/pause residue, so v2 is retired rather than rationalized.

## Selected candidate: dominant-field insert v3

v3 changes the hierarchy, not merely the seam. The compliant field is deliberately dominant; the controlled rigid field becomes a narrow asymmetric insert with a sloped outer edge and non-linear inner boundary. The result is one primary soft volume plus one controlled interface, rather than two equal panels. This maps directly to compliant surface first, rigid/control structure second.

| Route | Primary confusion | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| straight split field v1 | pause/control bars | reject |
| flowing equal-field seam v2 | two-panel/pause residue | reject |
| split capsule/pill | pharmaceutical/personal-care capsule | reject |
| four-zone aperture | app grid/navigation or medical zoning | reject |
| M1 monogram | generic technology monogram | reject |
| dominant-field insert v3 | one dominant mass plus controlled insert | select as digital candidate |

Observable criteria: one field must be materially dominant; the negative seam remains open and non-linear; no long parallel central edges; no platform container in canonical geometry; two-color/mono geometry remains identical; favicon/app/social/OG share `dominant-field-insert-v3`; web/app canonical files remain byte-identical; final 16/20/24/32 px device review remains mandatory before public launch. These are design criteria, not consumer-recognition or legal-distinctiveness claims.

Visual distinctiveness does not resolve the separate `MASCK` naming risk. Public records include US serial 79306463, International Registration 1580707 and Australian application lead 2142822. Official class/jurisdiction review and qualified trademark advice remain a hard launch gate. No infringement or clearance conclusion is made.
