## Commercial-burden corrections and additions

### Dyson Australia return and service expectation

Current Dyson Australia public material advertises free 45-day returns for direct online/in-store purchasing and a two-year parts-and-labour warranty, alongside Australian service centres and repair pathways. This supersedes the earlier Cell 4 benchmark wording that described a 30-day Dyson direct-return period.

Sources checked 2026-09-01:
- https://www.dyson.com.au/newsroom/new-premium-dyson-service-centre-brisbane-queensland
- https://www.dyson.com.au/dyson-stores-and-service-centres
- https://www.dyson.com.au/footer-primary-links/guarantee-terms

Masck One interpretation: support design must assume buyers of premium hardware can reasonably expect explicit fault triage, repair/replacement handling and a legible return pathway. This evidence does not prescribe a Masck One return period.

### Apple battery service expectation

Apple Australia states that rechargeable batteries have finite life and provides battery-service pathways; Apple Watch batteries are described as designed to retain up to 80 percent of original capacity at 1,000 complete charge cycles, with actual behaviour dependent on use. Apple also publishes multi-year post-sale parts/service availability expectations for supported products.

Sources checked 2026-09-01:
- https://www.apple.com/au/batteries/service-and-recycling/
- https://support.apple.com/en-au/102772

Masck One interpretation: battery ageing, serviceability and replacement policy belong in the commercial architecture. Apple data must not be used to infer Masck One cell life, runtime or cycle life.

### Eight Sleep recurring-burden and warranty coupling

Eight Sleep currently states that its standard and extended Pod warranty require an active membership during the applicable coverage period, with a two-year standard warranty and five-year extended warranty available under qualifying membership. Its Australian site also advertises a 30-night trial and direct return pathway.

Sources checked 2026-09-01:
- https://www.eightsleep.com/warranty/
- https://www.eightsleep.com/au/terms-and-conditions/

Masck One interpretation: linking core hardware support to a recurring subscription creates a meaningful ownership burden and error-recovery dependency. Current Masck One architecture therefore retains the preference that core cleanse, stop, fault-clear and service functions are locally operable and not subscription-gated unless a later, explicit safety case demonstrates otherwise.

Public evidence checked 2026-09-01:

Unknowns that remain mandatory before advancement: exact proposed site; consumer-wellness program fit; minimum engagement size; exact thin-section capability for the controlled geometry; tooling ownership; cosmetic surface capability; material lot documentation; fluid/cleanser compatibility evidence; exact inspection capability; subcontracted processes; NRE/MOQ/forecast requirements.

Advance path: non-confidential capability call -> NDA/IP boundary -> representative material/process requirements and non-sensitive geometry -> request exact site/process map, DFM plan, tooling model and CTQ inspection proposal -> keep REFERENCE_CANDIDATE until product-specific qualification evidence exists.

## Revised practical producer sequence

The current evidence supports a two-track sequence rather than prematurely selecting one whole-device manufacturer.

1. For Australia-accessible integrated NPI, retain Planet Innovation as the first local CDMO discussion candidate once the digital-alpha package is mature enough. Current public evidence confirms Melbourne/Irvine manufacturing, NPI, ISO 13485-compliant systems, consumable manufacturing and leak-testing capability. Source: https://planetinnovation.com/manufacturing/ and https://planetinnovation.com/capabilities/new-product-introduction/.
2. In parallel, use Trelleborg Medical Solutions as the highest-priority specialist process challenge for silicone/compliant-interface plus thermoplastic DFM. This is specifically to test whether the compliant/rigid architecture can be simplified and manufactured robustly, not to nominate Trelleborg as approved whole-device CM.
3. Keep Circuitwise as the primary Australia-based electronics NPI reference. Current public evidence confirms PCBA, DFM, inspection, product integration and ISO 13485 systems; the published ISO 13485 certificate scope identifies contract manufacture of electronic assemblies and associated cables for medical devices. Source: https://circuitwise.com.au/.
4. Keep Reid Print Technologies as a bounded HMI/flexible-electronics specialist reference for membrane interfaces, flexible sensors and heater/sensing coupons where later architecture authorizes them. Source: https://reidprinttechnologies.com.au/ and https://reidprinttechnologies.com.au/downloads/.
5. After alpha CTQs, test methods, assembly sequence and pilot quantities exist, compare Planet Innovation with Phillips Medisize, Jabil, Flex and Tessy-class scale paths using exact proposed sites and responsibilities. Brand scale alone is not a qualification criterion.

## Battery / compliance test reference update

SGS Australia currently advertises battery testing against standards including IEC 62133 and UN 38.3-related transportation testing. UL Solutions publicly lists UL/IEC 62133-2 lithium-system battery safety testing. These are test-laboratory/pathway references only, not a declaration of the exact final Masck One compliance scope.

Sources checked 2026-09-01:
- https://www.sgs.com/en-au/services/battery-and-accumulators
- https://www.ul.com/services/battery-safety-testing

Required later action: once battery architecture, pack responsibility, target markets and transport configuration are controlled, obtain a lab scoping review for the exact product and pack. Do not infer certification from using a pre-certified cell alone.

## Evidence-state firewall

Public market evidence may create PUBLIC_MARKET_BENCHMARK records only. Public supplier capability pages may create PUBLIC_SUPPLIER_CAPABILITY records only. Neither class may create a Masck One measurement or supplier approval.

A recurring-burden value can become MEASURED only when a controlled Masck One product-measurement artifact exists. A supplier can become APPROVED only after exact site, exact process scope and controlled supplier-qualification evidence are bound. The executable contract enforcing these transitions is `src/masck_one/commercial_evidence.py`.

No evidence in this addendum supports the phrases leak-proof, universal cleanser, exact runtime, exact cartridge cadence, exact refill cadence, orientation-independent, maintenance-free, skin-safe temperature, guaranteed efficacy, waterproof electronics, production-ready or regulatory-approved.
