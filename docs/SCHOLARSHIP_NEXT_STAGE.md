# Scholarship next-stage evidence plan

[Scholarship review snapshot](SCHOLARSHIP_REVIEW_SNAPSHOT.md) · [Venture progress](VENTURE_PROGRESS.md) · [Customer discovery](CUSTOMER_DISCOVERY.md) · [Evidence guide](EVIDENCE_GUIDE.md)

Masck One is currently much stronger in inspectable digital development than in customer, physical or commercial validation. The next stage should close that gap before the broader wearable-and-dock vision is allowed to drive additional scope.

This is an evidence plan, not a claim that the work below has already been completed.

## The next six evidence steps

| Step | Question | Minimum useful output | Decision enabled |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Structured customer interviews | Which routine problem is repeated and important enough to justify automation? | Interview records and a synthesis of recurring friction, current workarounds, objections and reasons people would not change behaviour. | Select, narrow or reject the first-use-case hypothesis. |
| Focused simulation and analysis | Which high-risk assumptions can be bounded digitally before hardware spend? | Decision records that identify what analysis can resolve and which questions still require measurement. | Reduce the physical test scope to the smallest useful experiments. |
| Focused physical fit prototype | Does the selected wearable interaction create a workable fit and wearing workflow? | Measured fit and workflow observations against a narrow test question. | Continue, redesign or reject the selected wearable path. |
| Core fluid-delivery testing | Can the required fluid behaviour be demonstrated under controlled physical testing? | Repeatable test records for the selected delivery and recovery question. | Continue, redesign or reject the fluid approach. |
| Hygiene and cleaning investigation | Does preparation and cleaning erase the intended convenience benefit? | Documented cleaning workflow, burden observations and identified sanitation questions. | Simplify the wet system, change servicing assumptions or stop an impractical path. |
| Initial manufacturing-cost modelling | Can plausible product complexity and cost overlap with demonstrated customer value? | A first model using sourced component, process and assembly assumptions. | Continue, reduce scope, change architecture or reconsider the commercial model. |

## Evidence before scope

The first product is deliberately not locked. The long-term direction is a wearable-and-dock system that could automate selected parts of a skincare routine, but that vision should not be treated as the first-product specification.

A narrower use case should earn focused physical development only when customer evidence identifies a repeated problem and the required fit, fluid, cleaning and cost questions can be tested without building the complete vision. Weak evidence should narrow or change the concept rather than trigger more features.

## How scholarship support converts into evidence

Scholarship support would be most useful where it replaces assumptions with reviewable evidence:

| Support | Evidence created |
| --- | --- |
| Customer-validation support | Structured interview records and a stronger basis for choosing the first use case. |
| Prototype materials and test equipment | Focused fit, fluid and cleaning measurements against defined questions. |
| Manufacturing and cost investigation | Sourced process, component and assembly assumptions for an initial cost model. |
| Specialist human collaboration | Practical challenge to founder and AI-assisted assumptions in hardware, manufacturing, IP, fundraising and market entry. |

No funding amount, physical result or commercial outcome is assumed. A more polished presentation or a more complete-looking prototype is not treated as evidence by itself.

## Current customer evidence boundary

The repository records a founder-reported informal survey of around 20 people. The strongest reported interest was around time and convenience, while comfort, maintenance and price were concerns. The underlying questionnaire, recruitment method and raw response data are not available in the repository, so this is preliminary and non-conclusive. It does not establish demand, willingness to pay or a validated customer segment.

## Founder and AI responsibility

AI is used extensively to accelerate implementation, research, alternative generation and exploration. Founder judgement owns product direction, requirements, priorities, trade-offs and final decisions.

The next stage also requires specialist human expertise and collaboration where digital work is insufficient, particularly in hardware, manufacturing, IP, fundraising and market entry. AI output, favourable analysis or a passing software check cannot upgrade a claim to physical or commercial validation.

## What success at the next stage means

Success is not completing all features in the long-term concept. It is reaching a defensible decision about whether one first use case deserves further development, supported by stronger customer evidence, focused physical measurements and an initial commercial reality check.
