# Do Not Confuse

- Core Sketch target ≠ validated hardware.
- Research benchmark ≠ MASCK specification.
- Product recognized ≠ product validated.
- Dispensed sunscreen amount ≠ proven SPF protection.
- CAD clearance ≠ physical comfort.
- Green CI ≠ buttery tactile feel.
- Community usage ≠ clinical/regulatory validation.
- AI confidence ≠ safety authority.
- More features ≠ better Masck.
