The non-negotiable hierarchy is **COMPLETE FACIAL ROUTINE -> cleaning + treatment + leave-on application + protection**. For a supported completed routine, the target end state is that when Masck One comes off, the user does not need to touch their face again to finish that facial skincare routine. Decorative cosmetics remain outside the V1 whole-routine promise, and facial SPF must never imply ears/neck/body protection.

A final whole-product/Fusion freeze is premature until the new complete-routine P0 concept and feasibility gates are reconciled. Green legacy subsystem CI is valuable evidence but is not sufficient by itself to freeze the expanded product.

Live engineering authority and accepted evidence always outrank product-concept documents on claims of what physically exists or works.
