# Iteration 20 acceptance: water reservoir architecture

Iteration 20 establishes a controlled fresh-water storage/service architecture without treating the earlier development envelope as closed internal geometry.

The authority requirements remain 6.5 mL gross and at least 5.5 mL usable. Stable fill, vent and pickup interfaces are defined, the selected service architecture is a user-removable refillable module, and its cavity is classified WET_REMOVABLE from the frozen hygiene vocabulary. Orientation test cases, pickup behavior, drain/dry intent, structural interface and leakage boundaries are explicit.

Internal volume and dead volume remain unresolved until controlled 3D storage geometry exists. A deterministic digital volume evaluator is provided so later generated geometry can establish internal minus dead volume and report target pass/fail without weakening the targets.

This release does not claim usable physical volume, leak performance, orientation robustness, vent performance, cleanability or drying performance.
