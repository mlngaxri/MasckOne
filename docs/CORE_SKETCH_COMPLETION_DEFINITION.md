# MASCK ONE Core Sketch Completion Definition

The product program must not use one vague “percent complete.” Completion exists at several layers, and the expanded complete-routine concept adds one stricter rule: **product completion is stage-by-region and session-specific**.

The detailed product-level predicates live in `CORE_SKETCH_P0_FEASIBILITY_CONTRACTS.md` and the canonical backlog contracts CS-015 through CS-018.

## Routine COMPLETE

A supported prepared routine may report `COMPLETE` only when:

- every mandatory stage in that exact prepared-session definition completed;
- every facial region required by each mandatory stage is complete or deliberately excluded by a justified, versioned product/claim boundary;
- no required region remains unreachable, unsupported, interrupted or unknown;
- exact required products/application profiles remain eligible;
- prepared-session identity, service history and resource state remain valid;
- required settling completed;
- normal release preserved the required final leave-on film.

A scalar average coverage percentage, successful cycle counter, completed pump command, green subsystem CI result or app state cannot override an unmet condition above.

Emergency/unpowered release remains safety-first. It may interrupt skincare completion rather than being delayed to preserve film.

## READY

`READY` is a derived property of the exact prepared session, not a cached light/UI flag.

It requires the selected routine/version, product identities/evidence, prepared doses, product-preservation/changeover state, water, waste capacity, energy, required thermal state, completed service state, local execution data and relevant fault state all to remain eligible **now**.

A material change invalidates or re-evaluates readiness. Network/cloud loss alone must not brick an otherwise valid locally prepared routine.

## Core Sketch complete

- selected product intent documented;
- rejected feature creep documented;
- canonical P0 contracts CS-015..018 defined;
- backlog covers all major product surfaces;
- shared interfaces have one writing owner;
- future changes have change-control path;
- physical unknowns remain explicitly PROVE/BLOCKED rather than being promoted by documentation.

Core Sketch completeness means the intended product and proof program are specified. It does **not** mean the product is feasible or validated.

## P0 feasibility-contract complete

- required-region completion semantics are explicit;
- every face-facing contact/support class is represented in the occlusion/transition contract;
- prepared-session validity and invalidation semantics are explicit;
- third-party product identity, physical characterisation, compatibility, contamination and application validation are separated;
- one whole-routine resource envelope covers fluids, mass/CG/torque, energy, time, dock/session storage and service;
- hostile logical invariants are machine-checked without promoting concept logic to engineering authority.

This layer can be closed digitally while the corresponding BENCH/HUMAN/REG evidence remains open.

## Reduced-region feasibility complete

- repeatable measurement methods exist for coverage, carryover/recovery and final-film survival;
- representative cheek-class curvature can execute CLEAN -> RINSE/RECOVER -> thin leave-on -> thicker leave-on/moisturiser -> final leave-on -> SETTLE -> non-wiping release;
- previously occluded test regions are finished rather than hidden by an average metric;
- protected-boundary analogs do not show uncontrolled migration;
- results repeat across selected representative product-property families and geometric/contact variations;
- limitations and uncertainty are recorded.

The detailed BENCH path is in `CORE_SKETCH_REDUCED_REGION_PROOF_PACKAGE.md`. Passing it does not establish whole-face or human-use completion.

## Digitally integrated

- current canonical owner outputs coexist in one assembly/system model;
- routine phases, CS-015 required-region map, CS-018 contact states, prepared-session/resource states, protected anatomy, mass/CG and service states are digitally represented with source provenance;
- the S0 minimum, S1 normal-PM and S2 demanding-AM routine resource scenarios have real or explicitly blocked inputs rather than convenient zeros;
- no known gross contradictions are hidden;
- current authority/source identities are consumed directly rather than copied concept values being treated as release truth.

## Prototype complete

- a physical full-face unit can execute at least one supported complete routine autonomously;
- every required facial region for that routine has credible completion evidence;
- user does not manually finish the face;
- normal release preserves the intended final layer;
- emergency/unpowered release remains independently available;
- device/dock resource and service loop returns to a truthful next-use state.

## Experience complete

- representative users can place/use/remove/service it successfully;
- comfort, vision, tactility, acoustics, hygiene confidence and post-removal finish meet selected targets;
- routine and ownership attention actually reduce the friction Masck is intended to remove;
- failure, partial-routine and offline states are understood without false completion confidence.

## Capability validated

- cleansing, application, optical, thermal and facial-SPF capabilities each have their required evidence independently;
- ordinary leave-on success does not automatically validate sunscreen;
- unsupported capabilities/products are not marketed as complete;
- product claims do not exceed the exact validated region/product/application envelope.

## Production-intent complete

- DFM, material qualification, tolerance, reliability, suppliers, compliance, service and claims evidence support the actual release candidate;
- product-preservation requirements for real third-party formulations are qualified;
- battery, fluid-contact materials, contact surfaces, seals and service components have the required production/supplier evidence;
- the released whole-product source/evidence package matches the physical candidate being claimed.

No lower layer should be described with the language of a higher layer.
