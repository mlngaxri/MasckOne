# Scholarship support to evidence

Masck One is pre-commercialisation. Scholarship support would be used to replace important assumptions with inspectable evidence, not to make the venture appear more mature than it is. No budget is asserted because the repository does not contain a supported budget.

| Support area | Evidence it should enable | Decision it should inform |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Structured customer validation | Comparable interview records about existing routines, recurring friction, workarounds and objections | Whether a narrow first use case is worth advancing, changing or rejecting |
| Prototype materials and test resources | Focused physical fit and workflow observations with conditions and limitations recorded | Whether the wearable interaction is practical enough to justify deeper hardware work |
| Fluid test resources | Repeatable core fluid-delivery observations, including failed runs and test conditions | Whether the selected use case has a credible physical delivery path or needs redesign |
| Hygiene and cleaning investigation | Recorded servicing and cleaning observations | Whether maintenance burden cancels the intended convenience benefit |
| Manufacturing and cost investigation | Sourced process assumptions and an initial sensitivity-based cost model | Whether the surviving scope has a plausible commercial path and which cost drivers dominate |

Focused simulation and analysis remain useful where justified inputs exist. They are not substitutes for customer or physical evidence.

Receiving support would not establish customer demand, product safety, comfort, population fit, fluid performance, hygiene, manufacturability or unit economics. Those claims require their own evidence.

The intended sequence is evidence-led: customer work should earn focused physical testing; focused physical evidence should earn deeper architecture work; customer value plus sourced manufacturing-cost evidence should earn broader product scope. A weak result should narrow, redirect or stop the relevant use case rather than trigger more feature development.

AI is used extensively to accelerate implementation, exploration, documentation and code-based CAD. I retain responsibility for venture direction, requirements, priorities, trade-offs and final decisions. Specialist human expertise and collaboration remain important for hardware, manufacturing, IP, fundraising and market entry.
