## 1. Verdict and surviving architecture

**The Core Sketch still describes one coherent product. Its physical feasibility is a conditional hypothesis, not a closed result.** The coherent unit is a prepared, untethered, complete facial routine plus the dock that prepares it. Cleaning, massage, optical treatment and thermal functions remain subordinate capabilities. A dock is necessary because isolation, cleaning and product preservation are likely to cost more package volume than the session liquids themselves.

Continue a bounded feasibility program. Do not freeze a production assembly or fund broad optional-feature refinement before the existential gates below. This review specifies off-face inert-surrogate evidence and engineering contracts. Human-use thermal, optical, cosmetic, barrier, comfort and protection protocols remain the responsibility of qualified validation owners; no operating recipe or human test authorization is supplied here.

Retain these architectural principles:

- Dock-side bulk product, isolated wearable session quantities, no routine tether.
- Contact, leave-on and release as required interface states; their mechanism remains unselected.
- Required-region application and final-film preservation define completion, not a pump counter.
- Optional contact/thermal/optical treatment normally precedes final leave-ons, subject to a validated sequence. Sharing a component must not force incompatible functions to operate together.
- A versioned prepared-session contract links physical contents, routine intent, machine capability and readiness. The primary control starts that eligible session locally.
- Unpowered emergency release remains independent of routine software and film preservation.
- Existing owner lineages, protected geometry, evidence boundaries, calm CMF and cost-conscious tactile design survive.

The five-lane split is retained. Reverting to component-only lanes would leave cross-stage contamination and completion without an owner. The correction is one producer per shared artifact, not a sixth lane or several competing integration registries.

## 3. Completion and readiness contracts

These are concept-level contracts for Lane 4, not executable safety software or new engineering limits.

**Required-region contract:** each selected stage names its required facial domain, prohibited domain, application method, accepted coverage/film criteria, permitted fit/hardware range and evidence basis. Geometric visibility, commanded dispensing and a validated application method are separate evidence types. Do not promise direct real-time film measurement unless such sensing exists and has been qualified.

**Prepared session:** bind routine revision, deliberate temporary overrides, exact product identities/versions, physical slot association, preparation receipt, dose state, storage/hold history, service completion, hardware/profile revisions, waste capacity, energy/thermal availability and required settle conditions. Bounds that require measurement remain UNKNOWN rather than receiving invented defaults.

**Completion predicate:** all required stages of that selected eligible session have credible execution evidence within their validated context; no unresolved dose, coverage, contamination or fault condition exists; required settling and the validated normal release conditions are met. A deliberately modified eligible session can complete, but must not be represented as completion of the unchanged saved routine. An unavailable required SPF stage cannot yield COMPLETE for an SPF-containing morning routine.

**Interruption:** preserve known stage progress and uncertainty. Resume only if the remaining sequence and physical contents are explicitly eligible. A command log cannot prove how much fluid moved during power loss. Unknown partial delivery must not be replayed as an “exactly once” software transaction.

**Time boundary:** completing this facial session does not eliminate later label-required reapplication or care, nor provide ears/neck/body protection. This distinction preserves the user promise without implying all-day protection. [FDA sunscreen guidance](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/understanding-over-counter-medicines/sunscreen-how-help-protect-your-skin-sun) distinguishes use conditions and reapplication from the product's SPF value.

## 8. Conditions to stop or materially pivot

Escalate an explicit concept decision if evidence shows any of the following persists across materially different plausible architectures:

1. Required facial regions cannot be finished without violating protected anatomy or being disturbed during support transfer/release.
2. Carryover, product preservation or final-film integrity cannot be controlled across a commercially meaningful range of the user's ordinary products.
3. Credible compatibility requires proprietary reformulation, recurring manual facial finishing or a narrower routine than the advertised complete promise.
4. The joint wearable constraints, fault containment and dependable unpowered release cannot coexist without changing the product's identity or engineering requirements.
5. Dock/refill/changeover/cleaning burden, wear inconvenience and cost erase the benefit over the manual routine for the intended cohort.
6. The intended morning SPF use cannot earn its required evidence. That blocks the SPF-containing AM proposition; an evening-only or other launch is a material scope decision, not a silent substitute.

One failed cam, pump, thermal candidate or delivery topology is not a concept kill. A disproportionate optional modality should be removed before disturbing the core. If the remaining obstacles are genuinely physical unknowns, stop digital optimization at the evidence boundary and obtain the appropriate qualified test, not another attractive render.

## 9. External evidence boundary

FDA identifies packaging, preservation and storage among factors in cosmetic microbial contamination. The design inference here is that transfer into Masck-controlled storage needs its own evidence rather than inheriting the retail container's assumptions. This source does not validate any proposed reservoir or service method. [FDA cosmetic microbiological safety](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/potential-contaminants-cosmetics/microbiological-safety-and-cosmetics).

Sunscreen claims need a dedicated jurisdiction/product/application review. [TGA therapeutic sunscreen guidance](https://www.tga.gov.au/resources/guidance/understanding-regulation-therapeutic-sunscreens) is a starting source for the existing Australian claims task, not a legal determination or approval of Masck. The preserved research archive remains exploration unless an individual claim is backed by an identified primary source or measured result.
