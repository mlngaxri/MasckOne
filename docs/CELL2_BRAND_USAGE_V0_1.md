# Masck One brand usage v0.1

Status: controlled digital candidate, not final trademark clearance and not a production CMF or hardware-marking release.

## Mark concept

The selected candidate uses two asymmetric soft engineered fields divided by one controlled flowing seam. The left field represents compliant contact behavior and the right field represents controlled rigid structure. The seam is the identity-bearing element: it changes direction through the vertical axis, remains open at small sizes and must not be filled, bridged or regularized into two equal bars.

The first straight-seam version is retired. Hostile review showed that two parallel rounded fields could collapse toward a generic pause/control glyph at favicon scale. The current candidate keeps the two-field product grammar but introduces unequal inner curvature and offset seam shoulders while preserving a calm outer envelope.

Routes considered:

1. Flowing split-field seam. Current candidate because it derives directly from compliant-versus-rigid product grammar, remains legible without text and avoids face, eye, medical and VR symbolism.
2. Straight split-field seam. Retired because it could read as a generic pause/control glyph at small sizes.
3. Four-zone aperture. Rejected for primary identity because a quadrant/grid construction reads too close to generic software navigation or medical zoning, despite reflecting the four cleansing zones.
4. M1 monogram. Rejected for primary identity because it depends on typography, is less product-specific and creates a higher risk of generic technology-brand behavior.

## Observable criteria

- canonical symbol source uses two filled paths and one continuous negative-space seam;
- the seam must visibly change direction through the mark rather than remain a straight parallel gap;
- the two fields must not resolve as equal-width or equal-shape bars;
- no face, eye, cross, shield, sparkle, droplet or ring silhouette;
- no gradient, blur, shadow, baked platform mask or simulated material effect in canonical source;
- at 16, 20, 24 and 32 px the negative seam must remain visibly open;
- monochrome and reversed treatments must preserve the exact seam topology;
- web and app canonical `brand-mark.svg` files must remain byte-identical;
- source mark may appear in controlled green plus ink, or as the monochrome source; do not recolor individual lobes independently;
- minimum clear space around a standalone mark is one eighth of the symbol box width;
- do not place the mark inside an additional arbitrary rounded rectangle except where an operating system, browser or export format requires a container;
- do not rotate, skew, outline, add facial apertures or combine the mark with unverified product geometry.

## Small-scale confusion checks

The mark must be rejected or revised if, in blinded small-size review, it reads primarily as any of the following before it reads as its own abstract symbol:

- pause or media-control bars;
- a split pill/capsule;
- two generic app panels;
- a medical cross or facial/eye motif;
- a monogram created accidentally by the negative space.

This is a visual-design criterion only. It is not a legal distinctiveness opinion.

## Palette use

The digital candidate inherits released Cell 2 surface colors: field `#e8e5dc`, ink `#1d211f`, controlled green `#314f38`. These are digital presentation colors only and must not be represented as frozen physical CMF.

## Asset roles

- `brand-mark.svg`: canonical two-color symbol source.
- `brand-mark-mono.svg`: one-color source for monochrome or reversed export. White reversal is an export treatment of this geometry, not separate geometry.
- `favicon.svg`: browser-scale composition with field background. Its rounded container is presentation-only and not part of the canonical mark.
- `app-icon-source.svg`: 1024 by 1024 unmasked source composition. Platform masks and rendering effects are applied downstream using current platform tooling.
- `social-avatar-source.svg`: square social-profile source using the same path geometry and optical placement as the app-icon source.
- `og-source.svg`: editable social-card source. Its development disclaimer is mandatory until claims governance releases stronger public copy.

## Wordmark

The current interface wordmark remains typographic `MASCK ONE`. A custom outlined wordmark is intentionally not frozen in v0.1 because final production typography has not been locked. Do not convert the current system-font rendering into permanent logo outlines.

## Legal status

The product name is not trademark-cleared. Current evidence includes the standard-character mark `MASCK`, US serial 79306463, International Registration 1580707, with listed software and skin/medical assessment goods/services. A public Australian index also points to Australian application 2142822 across classes including 009, 016, 042 and 044; that status must be confirmed in official IP Australia records.

This record does not establish infringement, registrability or likelihood of confusion. It does establish a material adjacency that makes formal review a hard gate before public launch, paid acquisition, packaging production, manufacturing mark application or expensive final brand rollout.

Before those activities: confirm the Australian record in ATMS, search similar words/phonetic variants and relevant classes in intended jurisdictions, map the actual Masck One goods/services against cited registrations/applications, and obtain qualified trademark advice. Do not describe `MASCK ONE` as cleared until that process is complete.
