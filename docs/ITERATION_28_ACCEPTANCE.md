# Iteration 28 acceptance: complete fresh/waste routing closure

Iteration 28 binds the four controlled fresh-fluid route interfaces and two controlled waste-route interfaces into one deterministic, provenance-bearing route ledger. It closes route identity and subsystem ownership while preserving the evidence firewall established by Iterations 22, 26 and 27.

No controlled centerlines, selected tubing inner diameters, supplier minimum bend radii, achieved bend radii, connector geometry, or assembly/service trajectories exist in the verified baseline. Consequently, numeric centerline length, bend-radius compliance, dead volume and service clearance remain unset. Hydraulic performance remains validation-gated pending controlled geometry, fluid properties and pump curves. The closure rejects attempts to fill those fields speculatively or promote their statuses.

This is intentionally stronger than a superficial numeric ledger: dead volume is not calculated from guessed tube dimensions, and bend-radius compliance is not asserted against a guessed supplier limit. Once controlled centerlines and tubing evidence exist, those values can be added as measured/derived inputs and checked without changing the route identities or evidence semantics.

Iteration 28 is digital topology closure only. It is not hydraulic validation, purge/prime validation, mixed-phase recovery evidence, leakage evidence, serviceability evidence, hygiene evidence or physical performance evidence.
