# MASCK ONE Core Sketch Non-Negotiables

1. Complete supported facial skincare routine in one wear session.
2. No required manual facial skincare after normal completion.
3. User’s supported existing skincare, not mandatory MASCK chemistry.
4. Bulk products in dock; only session quantities on-head.
5. Calm premium personal-care exterior, not VR/PPE/medical/gaming/robotic.
6. Large clean eyes, real airway, protected anatomy preserved.
7. Quiet, restrained sensory language; no activity theater.
8. Non-wiping leave-on application and final release.
9. Offline normal use and power-independent emergency removal.
10. AI subordinate to deterministic safety/application bounds.
11. Hygiene must be inspectable and credible.
12. Makeup remains outside V1 complete-routine promise.
13. Facial SPF requires separate validation and never implies body/neck/ear coverage.
14. Physical claims require physical evidence; digital work cannot promote them by wording.
