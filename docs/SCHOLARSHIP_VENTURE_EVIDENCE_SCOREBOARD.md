## Decision rule

Repository activity demonstrates initiative and creates testable artefacts. It does not by itself earn broader product scope.

Customer evidence should earn a focused first-use-case test. Focused physical evidence should earn deeper hardware development. Customer value plus sourced manufacturing-cost evidence should earn a tighter commercial product definition. Weak or contradictory evidence should narrow, change or reject the relevant path rather than be hidden by additional features.

## What scholarship support would accelerate

Scholarship support would be most useful where it converts assumptions into inspectable evidence: structured customer validation; prototype materials and test equipment for fit, fluid and cleaning questions; and manufacturing and cost investigation. No funding amount, technical outcome or commercial result is assumed.

AI is used extensively to accelerate implementation, research and exploration. I retain responsibility for direction, requirements, priorities, trade-offs and final decisions. Specialist human expertise and collaboration remain important for hardware, manufacturing, IP, fundraising and market entry.

## Evidence boundary

Masck One remains pre-commercialisation. The repository does not currently establish customer demand, willingness to pay, comfort, population fit, hygiene practicality, real fluid performance, safety, manufacturing feasibility, viable unit economics or product-level physical performance. The long-term wearable-and-dock direction should not be read as a committed or already-working product specification.
