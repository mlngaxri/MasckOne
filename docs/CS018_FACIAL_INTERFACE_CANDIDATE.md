# CS-018 reduced-region interface candidate

Status: selected **unpowered off-face inert-surrogate experiment**, not a released wearable architecture. CS-018 and whole-face CS-015 remain BLOCKED. This Lane 1 owner consumes the released Core Sketch and subsystem sources read-only. It does not implement Routine OS, product identity, readiness, dosing software or human-use treatment.

## Architecture decision

| Family | Decision | Governing reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alternating facial support sets | Rejected as final-phase baseline | Moving the last support between required regions does not eliminate its last untreated footprint. It still needs independent reaction or evidence that contact preserves the required layer. More contaminated footprints also increase carryover risk. |
| Rolling combined contact/applicator | Not selected | Cleaning-contact reuse and rolling over earlier layers introduce unproved carryover, pickup and shear. Broad product-family compatibility cannot follow from a moving roller. |
| Independent reaction, normal lift, catch shutter, separate underpassing delivery interface | Selected for reduced-region experiment | It separates structural reaction, contaminated contact, drip interception and final-region access. It tests the central topology without pretending cranial support has been solved. |

A projected shadow need not move sideways if a separate application path can reach **beneath** it. Normal retraction alone is insufficient: the underpassing path, reaction path and residue interception must also exist. The fixture embodies those three missing relationships.

The current thermal owner has two exact planar contact faces of 616 mm² each. Each intersects 383 released development mesh triangles in the reproduced audit. Pure Z translation preserves that XY projection. The five protected-prism intersections per plate are zero in this limited plate audit; this is neither whole-product clearance nor registered anatomical evidence. Source heads, file blobs and thermal STEP hashes are recorded in the JSON snapshot and generated audit.

## Actual embodiment and states

Fourteen separate positive B-reps: inert curved coupon; external reaction base; contact cassette incorporating peripheral ring and central island; two normal guides; two parking pins; catch shutter; two shutter guides; separate delivery body and lid; two transverse delivery guides.

| State | Contact cassette | Catch shutter | Independent delivery interface | Reaction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CONTACT | Coupon-matched face | Parked outside target | Parked | External base and coupon flange |
| LIFTED | Normal clearance established | Parked | Parked | Same external reaction; cassette not yet pin captured |
| ISOLATED | Positively pin captured | Between contact cassette and target | May traverse below shutter | External base, guides and pin capture |
| SERVICE | Removed along normal guide direction | Remains over target | Parked | External base; pins withdrawn |

The 30 × 40 mm observation domain is divided into twelve individually measured curved cells. Every cell intersects the delivery-access volume fully. This proves a geometric access window, **not** deposition, dose, spreading, cleaning, preservation or completeness. Required regions cannot be inferred from a global average. The curved coupon is an inert engineering surrogate, not a facial fit model.

Continuous references conservatively enclose normal lift, pin acquisition, shutter translation, delivery scan and cassette withdrawal. Installed material intersections are checked separately in all states. Reference solids never enter manufacturing assemblies. The withdrawal is a fixture service motion, not whole-head removal. Emergency wearable release remains with the retention owner and is never gated on successful film preservation here.

Guidance and relieved mating are geometry choices, not evidence of low friction, precision feel or noise.

## Reduced-region evidence hooks

This is an off-face mechanical surrogate fixture, not instructions for treatment on a person. Record each cell before and after each simulated state; track contact footprints, access, deposited surrogate distribution and displaced/collected surrogate mass separately. Inspect the cassette underside and catch shutter for cross-transfer. Record any recontact, blocked cell, pooling or bypass of the shutter. Compare the initial and final cell maps rather than only total recovered mass. Any required cell unresolved or materially disturbed blocks regional success.

Bench work must establish contamination isolation and film survival independently of geometry. Broad thin/thick application-family claims remain unvalidated. SPF is a separate REG/CLAIM proof class and cannot be promoted from ordinary surrogate deposition. A successful coupon experiment permits only evaluation of a larger representative rig, not whole-face completion.

## Remaining P0 order

1. DIGITAL: registered owner footprints and a wearable reaction path outside all required final-layer regions; package and joint resource feasibility.
2. DIGITAL: continuous complete-device normal release and all face-side application paths on that registration.
3. BENCH: carryover, residue interception, distribution and layer survival by individual region and product family.
4. HUMAN: fit, stability, comfort and controlled removal through qualified testing.
5. REG/CLAIM: facial SPF and any treatment claims; SUPPLIER/PRODUCTION: materials, wear, seals, fixation and manufacturing capability.

If independent reaction cannot coexist with required-region access, protected anatomy and mass/torque, redesign the shared interface. If validated distribution or film preservation fails across practical families, narrow support explicitly. If completion requires the user to finish required facial regions manually, the locked complete-routine proposition has not been achieved; a concept decision is required rather than a COMPLETE flag.
