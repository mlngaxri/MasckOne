## Decision rule

The next stage should produce one of three outcomes for a candidate first use case: **advance**, **narrow or change**, or **reject**. More repository output by itself is not a reason to advance.

A decision should record the evidence that changed it, the assumptions that remain unresolved, and the cheapest next test capable of reducing the most important uncertainty. Negative evidence is useful when it prevents further investment in a weak direction.

## Role of scholarship support

Scholarship support would be used to improve these decisions, not to make the venture appear more mature than its evidence. Relevant uses include structured customer validation, prototype materials and test equipment for focused questions, manufacturing and cost investigation, and specialist human input where hardware, manufacturing, IP, fundraising or market-entry judgement is required.

No budget, traction, physical validation, revenue, partnership or technical-performance claim is implied by this decision log.
