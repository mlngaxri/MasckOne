## Required digital conditions

1. The release boundary topology is bound to the current facial-surface identity, source-asset hash, registered-mesh SHA, registration revision, coverage segmentation and compliant-interface topology.
2. Coverage from a different rigid registration of the same source asset is rejected by the registered-mesh geometry identity gate.
3. Six source-region provenance partitions remain explicit: outer perimeter, left/right eye, mouth and left/right nostril.
4. Four physical material/no-material systems are present: outer perimeter, bilateral eye union, mouth and bilateral nostril union.
5. Each physical system is exactly one closed edge loop on the current development mesh. Individual bilateral provenance partitions are not falsely required to be closed when the conservative protected regions overlap.
6. Every protected-aperture edge separates one contact triangle from one matching source-labelled protected-region triangle.
7. No mesh edge belongs to more than one provenance boundary.
8. No protected region reaches the outer development perimeter unexpectedly.
9. No numeric transition width or general interface thickness is introduced without authority.
10. The authority eye inner-edge roll is preserved only as a rigid-edge design reference, not a compliant-profile parameter.
11. Neutral left/right eye and nostril provenance discretizations remain sagittally balanced.
12. The exported build manifest contains exact edge identities sufficient to reconstruct the digital transition topology.
13. The topology is deterministic and SHA-256 identified.
14. The topology remains ineligible for anatomical, seal, ingress, pressure, material or efficacy validation.
15. Existing authority validation, repository preflight, Iteration-11 nasal preflight, unit/integration tests and deterministic CAD smoke build continue to pass.
