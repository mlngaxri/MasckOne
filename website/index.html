A wearable system designed to deliver, move and recover cleansing fluid while your hands stay free.

01 / Object
Second page / form and contact

One object.
Two surfaces.

The rigid architecture carries the mechanism. A separate compliant interface handles contact with the face. Keeping those roles distinct makes fit, sealing and service access measurable rather than cosmetic.

ExteriorThin protective shell, protected openings and a serviceable perimeter.
InterfaceCompliant skin contact, eye clearance and dedicated airway geometry.
Product visuals are illustrative. Fit, sealing, airway performance, comfort and production readiness remain validation-gated.01 / 03
02 / System
02.1 / contained fluid circuit

Fresh in.
Used fluid out.

Water and cleanser move through controlled supply paths while used fluid is routed separately toward containment. The architecture is designed to be measured as a closed system.

SupplyMetered fresh-water and cleanser distribution.
ReturnSeparate acquisition path for recovered fluid.
Flow rate, recovery efficiency and leakage remain physical evidence gates.02.1
