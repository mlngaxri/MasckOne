Principle01
The product intent

Make cleansing a contained product experience.

MASCK ONE is being developed around a simple interaction goal: move the messy parts of facial cleansing into the product itself, then make the remaining customer actions obvious.

Design language

Quiet outside.
Busy inside.

The exterior is intentionally calm. The internal architecture carries the complexity: supply, controlled movement, recovery, retention, service access and power.

ContainedFluid handling is treated as one closed customer experience.
LegibleEach action should explain itself without a tutorial.
ServiceableRoutine access is designed into the product architecture.
Experience02
Customer sequence

Seven states. One continuous routine.

The current website journey follows the product architecture that exists today. Where hardware or physical validation is unresolved, the interface says so instead of inventing finished behavior.

01 / PREPARECurrent digital architecture
Prepare01 / 07

Prepare supply before wear.

Water and cleanser are prepared before the device goes to the face.

Fresh-water and cleanser service architecture exist digitally; finished customer refill closures and the complete service motion remain open.
Wear02 / 07

Place. Support. Settle.

The front shell is placed to the face, then the rear support geometry comes into position.

Current retention geometry is digital. Population fit, comfort, preload and hair interaction still require physical validation.
Choose03 / 07

Control stays deliberately simple.

The primary physical control area is reserved for choosing and activation without pretending the final HMI has already been frozen.

Control count, function mapping, switch technology, travel, force and tactile response remain unresolved.
Clean04 / 07

Supply. Move. Recover.

The cleansing cycle is described at customer level: provide fluid, move it across the treatment interface, then recover it.

The customer sequence intentionally avoids showing unvalidated internal routing detail as if it were finished hardware.
Remove05 / 07

Release, then lift away.

The current digital architecture reserves a right-side one-hand release direction for removal.

Release force, time, wet usability and complete whole-head removal remain physical-validation gates.
Service06 / 07

Routine access moves to the rear.

The rear service cover is the visible starting point for routine product service.

The visible rear cover belongs to the current exterior candidate. Internal service geometry remains digital and the complete customer motion remains open.
Recharge07 / 07

Power is reserved, not fictionalised.

The dry-side package reserves a left-lower charging interface location.

Connector type, retention, ingress and electrical validation remain unresolved, so no dock, cable or charge-time claim is shown.
Architecture03
System logic

The complexity belongs inside the product.

At the customer level, the current architecture is easiest to understand as three verbs. They stay intentionally abstract where engineering evidence has not closed the detail.

Contained cycle

Three verbs.
One loop.

Supply brings fresh fluid to the treatment interface. Move distributes the action across the face. Recover carries used fluid away from the interface.

Claim boundary05
Engineering status

Ambition is not the same thing as evidence.

MASCK ONE is an engineering-development project. The site is intentionally specific about what is digital architecture and what has not yet been physically validated. Refill closures, population fit, release performance, final HMI mapping, complete customer service motion and the charging connector remain unresolved.

Shown

Current digital exterior, retention, service-access, HMI-capacity and dry-side architecture where registered by the project.

Not claimed

No finished dock, connector, refill cap, bottle, validated fit range or physical release performance is presented as solved.

Next evidence

Physical fit, comfort, wet handling, release, ingress, service motion, manufacturing and electrical validation remain decisive.
