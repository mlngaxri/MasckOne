02 / HOW IT WORKS

Deliver.
Work.
Collect.

Three system responsibilities, shown as a development narrative rather than validated performance.

01

DELIVER

Meter fluid to the interface.

Fresh water and cleanser remain distinct upstream identities in the engineering architecture. Final realized routing geometry is still validation-gated.

02

WORK

Create controlled cleansing motion.

The current digital architecture reserves controlled actuation intent. Physical loaded motion, efficacy, comfort, acoustics, and durability are not yet public performance claims.

03

COLLECT

Move used fluid away from the face.

The waste architecture preserves mixed-phase collection through a pump and passive backflow barrier before the cartridge interface. Recovery and containment remain validation-gated.
